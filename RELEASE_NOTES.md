# Pour Toujours 1.0.0

Pour Toujours V1 is a privacy-first family awareness app for understanding shared time, weather, routines, birthdays, holidays, and plans across Karachi, Chiba, Dublin, and Hattiesburg.

## What is included

- Living Today dashboard with family-world context
- Interactive city experiences with hourly and seven-day weather
- Daylight, sunrise, sunset, and daylight-saving intelligence
- Official severe-weather context and tornado-warning prioritization
- Viewer-relative family relationships and rich person pages
- Explainable routine-based availability estimates
- Family overlap planner
- Monthly calendar, birthdays, holidays, and locally stored family events
- Three native Android home-screen widgets
- Opt-in local notifications with quiet hours
- Six theme collections with light, dark, and system modes
- Accessibility, privacy, attribution, offline, and stale-data experiences

## Privacy

The app does not use GPS, background location, call logs, messaging activity, WhatsApp presence, or live activity tracking. Availability is inferred from local time and routines entered in the app. Identity, routines, events, and reminder preferences stay on the device. Weather, holiday, and alert requests use city- or country-level public information.
